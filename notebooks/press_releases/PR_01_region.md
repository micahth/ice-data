# PRESS RELEASE 1 — Facility-located detention per resident rose in every region; the South leads and the Midwest grew fastest

**Old = CY2023–2024 · New = 2025-01-01 → 2026-03-11.** Rates are average daily population (ADP) per 100,000 residents.

| Region | Old ADP | New ADP | Old per 100k | New per 100k | Change | New book-ins/100k/yr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| South | 22,753 | 34,626 | 17.3 | 25.9 | +50% | 552 |
| West | 7,585 | 12,128 | 9.5 | 15.1 | +59% | 274 |
| Northeast | 2,349 | 4,665 | 4.1 | 8.0 | +95% | 148 |
| Midwest | 803 | 3,576 | 1.2 | 5.1 | **+318%** | 129 |

- The South holds 63% of the national average daily population and has 5× the Midwest's per-capita rate.
- By calendar year, South: 15.8 (2023) → 18.7 → 24.9 → 30.9 (2026 YTD).
- Median completed facility-stint duration fell (South 9.8 → 2.0 days; West 4.6 → 0.9). These are stint lengths, not full stays; ongoing stints are excluded, so the partial 2026 window is not directly comparable.

**Caveat:** ADP is computed from ICE book-in/out stints. Residents are Census county populations (2025 used for 2026).
This rate describes detention facilities located in each region; it does not estimate the share of that region's residents who are detained.
