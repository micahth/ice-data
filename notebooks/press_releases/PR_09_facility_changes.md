# PRESS RELEASE 9 — 111 facility codes appeared and 13 stopped since January 20, 2025; several private-prison sites were reactivated

- **New codes (111):** local/state jails (78+), ICE sites (14), federal (11), CoreCivic (5), GEO (2).
- **Largest:** Camp East Montana, El Paso (35.5k book-ins), the El Paso soft-sided facility (15.7k), Florida's "Alligator Alcatraz" (13.7k), Baker CI (5.7k), GEO's North Lake, MI (5.6k) and Folkston D. Ray, GA (5.3k), CoreCivic's Cimarron OK (4.3k) and California City (3.7k).
- **Stopped booking:** Alamance Co. NC, Dallas Co. TX, Gaston, Cabarrus, York SC and others.
- **Reported ownership/operator events** (California City, Diamondback, North Lake, Delaney Hall, Alligator Alcatraz) come from my own knowledge and are **unverified** here.
