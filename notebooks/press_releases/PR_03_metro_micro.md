# PRESS RELEASE 3 — Rural facilities have the highest detention rate per county resident: 78 per 100k vs 11 in metro areas

Facilities placed by county into OMB (July 2023) Metropolitan, Micropolitan, or non-CBSA (rural). Rate = ADP in those facilities ÷ residents of the same county group.

| Area type | Old per 100k | New per 100k | 2026 YTD | Median completed facility stint Old → New (days) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Metropolitan | 6.2 | 10.9 | 12.8 | 3.1 → 1.4 |
| Micropolitan | 20.0 | 30.6 | 35.3 | 24.7 → 11.7 |
| Rural (non-CBSA) | 54.9 | 78.4 | 99.3 | 21.5 → 12.5 |

Highest-rate areas (New, per 100k; ADP ≥ 500):

| Area | Old | New |
|---|---:|---:|
| Natchez, MS-LA | 3,237 | 4,387 |
| Raymondville, TX | 3,797 | 4,204 |
| DuBois, PA | 1,378 | 1,914 |
| Alamogordo, NM | 1,053 | 1,242 |
| Batavia, NY | 791 | 1,137 |
| Columbus, GA-AL | 424 | 596 |
| El Paso, TX | 78 | **236 (3×)** |

Metro areas account for 58% of the new ADP but have the lowest per-capita rates. Small towns with a single large facility show per-resident rates far above any large city.

**Caveat:** a facility's rate reflects detainees from across the country, not the locality's own residents.
Duration is measured per completed facility stint, not per full detention stay; ongoing stints are excluded.
