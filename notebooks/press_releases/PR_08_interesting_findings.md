# PRESS RELEASE 8 — Interesting findings

1. **Rural concentration:** the non-CBSA detention rate is 78 per 100k (99 in 2026 YTD), 7× metro areas.
2. **Natchez, MS-LA** holds more than 40 detainees per 1,000 residents (4,387 per 100k).
3. **El Paso tripled** (78 → 236 per 100k) as Camp East Montana (35.5k book-ins) and the soft-sided site (15.7k) opened.
4. **Midwest +318%** per capita; 74% of Midwest ADP is local/state jails.
5. **Local/state sites grew 3.5×** (2.2k → 7.8k ADP), the fastest-growing operator class; Florida's state-run sites helped push FL 2.6×.
6. **Completed facility stints were shorter, book-ins more numerous:** median stint duration 6.9 → 1.8 days; book-ins rose from 1.09M over 24 months to 1.36M over 15 months; ICE-run book-ins annualized 214k → 575k (hold rooms/staging). The raw unique-person totals (539k vs 455k) cover unequal periods and are not directly comparable. Stint duration is not full-stay duration and excludes ongoing stints.
7. **BOP and CBP facilities** went from ~22 to ~971 ADP.
8. **New facilities:** 111 facility codes first booked after 1/20/2025 (see `FACILITY_CHANGES.md`); 13 stopped booking.
9. **Regression:** detention is not robustly linked to unemployment or property crime; GDP and violent-crime signals are unstable.
10. **Detention is concentrated:** Louisiana (163/100k), NM (72), MS (65) are 3–10× the national rate.
