# PRESS RELEASE 5 — National detention rate up ~63%: 9.9 → 16.1 per 100,000 residents

| Measure | Old (2023–24) | New (2025–Mar 2026) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average daily population | 33.6k | 55.1k |
| ADP per 100k residents | ≈9.9 | ≈16.1 |
| Book-ins | 1.09M | 1.36M (15 months) |
| Unique people booked (period total) | 539k | 455k |
| Median completed facility stint (days) | 6.9 | 1.8 |

By year ADP: 2023 30.2k · 2024 36.9k · 2025 53.0k · 2026 YTD 65.9k.

Per-capita detail: regions (PR 1), states (PR 2), metro/micro/rural (PR 3), operators (PR 4).

**Read with care:** the periods are unequal (24 months vs 15 months), so raw unique-person totals are not directly comparable. Book-ins rose despite the shorter new period; completed facility stints were shorter (hold rooms and staging book-ins; ICE-run book-ins annualized 214k → 575k). Stint duration is not a full-stay measure: ongoing stints are excluded, and the partial 2026 window is not directly comparable.
